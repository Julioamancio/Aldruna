A 768x256 pixel-art WALL set for a top-down 2D MMORPG in the classic Tibia /
A Link to the Past style, on a solid flat magenta background (#FF00FF) that
becomes transparency. Hand-drawn pixel art, crisp, NOT a photo, NOT 3D.

This is the minimal 3-piece wall system. LAYOUT: exactly 3 cells in one row,
each cell 256x256 pixels, side by side, no gaps or lines between them.

The wall is grey cut-stone blocks with dark mortar, seen in Tibia's top-down
3/4 view: a thin lit band on TOP of the wall, a taller shadowed FRONT FACE
below it, a dark line at the very bottom where it meets the floor.
5-tone ramp, hue-shifted: cool dark #33383f in the gaps up to warm light
#b8bec8 on the top rim. Light from the north-west. Around each wall the cell is
flat pure magenta with a hard clean edge — no shadow or glow onto the magenta.

The three pieces (this exact order, left to right):

CELL 1 — HORIZONTAL WALL: a straight wall running left-to-right across the full
width of the cell. It must TILE horizontally: the stone pattern at the left
edge continues perfectly into the right edge, so a row of these forms one
unbroken wall. The wall fills the lower ~two thirds of the cell height; the top
third above the wall is magenta.

CELL 2 — VERTICAL WALL: a straight wall running top-to-bottom across the full
height of the cell. It must TILE vertically: the top edge continues into the
bottom edge. The wall fills the left ~two thirds of the cell width; the right
third is magenta. Show the wall's thickness (its left side face) so it reads as
seen slightly from the side.

CELL 3 — POLE / CORNER POST: a single square stone post that JOINS walls. It is
the connector placed where a horizontal and a vertical wall meet, and at
corners and crossings. It must line up with both the horizontal wall's height
band and the vertical wall's width band, so that horizontal + pole + vertical
form a clean right-angle corner with no overlap gap. Roughly fills the centre
of the cell, magenta around it.

The three pieces must be drawn at the SAME stone scale and the SAME lighting so
they look like one wall. A horizontal wall meeting a pole meeting a vertical
wall must form a seamless corner.

Strict: no text, no numbers, no watermark, no floor or grass under the walls
(only magenta), no characters, no props, no doors, no windows.
