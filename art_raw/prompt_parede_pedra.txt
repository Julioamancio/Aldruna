A 1024x1024 top-down RPG WALL tileset in the polished 16-bit style of
The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past and Secret of Mana. Hand-drawn pixel
art, crisp and colourful, NOT a photo, NOT 3D render, NOT blurry.

The whole sheet is drawn over a solid flat magenta background (#FF00FF) that
will become transparency. Around every wall piece the magenta is untouched,
with a hard clean edge — no shadow, glow or fade onto the magenta.

LAYOUT: a 4x4 grid, 16 cells, each cell exactly 256x256 pixels, holding one
wall piece. No gaps, no grid lines, no labels.

STYLE OF THE STONE (apply to every piece, keep it consistent):
- Wall built from stacked grey cut-stone blocks with dark mortar lines.
- 5-tone ramp, hue-shifted: cool dark #33383f in the deepest gaps, then
  #4f565f, #6b727c, #8b929c, up to a warm light #b8bec8 on the top rim.
- Each block has a lit top-left facet and a shadowed bottom-right facet, so
  the wall reads as chunky and solid, never flat.
- A bright 1-pixel highlight runs along the very top ridge; a dark line sits at
  the very bottom where the wall meets the ground.
- Subtle wear: a few chipped corners and hairline cracks, tastefully sparse.

PERSPECTIVE — this is the key: top-down 3/4 view, the same as classic Tibia.
The wall is seen slightly from the front-above, so its TOP surface is a thin
band and its FRONT FACE fills most of the cell below it. Walls have thickness
and cast their own form-shadow, but no shadow leaves the wall onto the magenta.

The 16 pieces must tile together seamlessly — a horizontal piece placed next
to a corner must line up perfectly. Reading order:
1.  horizontal wall segment, running east-west, full width, both ends open
2.  vertical wall segment, running north-south, full height, both ends open
3.  outer corner joining south + east
4.  outer corner joining south + west
5.  outer corner joining north + east
6.  outer corner joining north + west
7.  T-junction: horizontal wall with a branch going south
8.  T-junction: horizontal wall with a branch going north
9.  T-junction: vertical wall with a branch going east
10. T-junction: vertical wall with a branch going west
11. cross junction meeting from all four sides
12. horizontal wall ending in a finished cap on its west end
13. vertical wall ending in a finished cap on its north end
14. a single square stone pillar standing alone
15. horizontal wall with a closed arched WOODEN DOOR in the centre
16. horizontal wall with an arched BARRED WINDOW in the centre

Strict: no text, no numbers, no watermark, no characters, no floor or grass
under the walls (only magenta), no furniture, no plants, no torches.
