Create a 1024x1024 pixel art tile sheet for a 2D top-down MMORPG, arranged as a
4x4 grid of 16 square tiles (each tile is exactly 256x256 pixels). These are
DIRT PATH EDGE OVERLAY tiles: each tile shows the edge of a brown dirt path,
drawn over a solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF) which will be removed and
replaced by transparency.

Only the dirt itself is drawn; the rest of every tile must be flat pure magenta
with a hard edge - no fading, no shadow, no glow onto the magenta.

CRITICAL ALIGNMENT RULE: every shape must be positioned exactly as described
and must touch the tile edges it owns, reaching all the way to the border of
its 256x256 cell. Do not shrink, offset or center shapes that should touch the
edges.

The 16 tiles, in reading order:
1. dirt filling the top half, ragged horizontal edge across the middle
2. dirt filling the right half, ragged vertical edge
3. dirt filling the bottom half, ragged horizontal edge
4. dirt filling the left half, ragged vertical edge
5. dirt filling only the top-right quarter (outer corner, rounded)
6. dirt filling only the bottom-right quarter (outer corner)
7. dirt filling only the bottom-left quarter (outer corner)
8. dirt filling only the top-left quarter (outer corner)
9. dirt filling everything except the top-right quarter (inner corner)
10. dirt filling everything except the bottom-right quarter (inner corner)
11. dirt filling everything except the bottom-left quarter (inner corner)
12. dirt filling everything except the top-left quarter (inner corner)
13. horizontal dirt strip across the middle (a narrow trail)
14. vertical dirt strip down the middle (a narrow trail)
15. small round dirt patch in the centre, isolated
16. dirt edge with a few small pebbles and tiny grass tufts along the boundary

The dirt must match this palette exactly: #6b5334 #7d6240 #8a7350 #574327, with
small darker specks and tiny stones so the edge reads as crumbly packed earth,
slightly irregular, never a clean geometric line.

Lighting comes from the north-west: the north-west side is slightly lighter,
the south-east side slightly darker, but never pure black.

Style: crisp pixel art, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no
gradients, flat lighting. Strict rules: no text, no labels, no numbers, no
watermark, no grid lines between tiles, no drop shadows, no characters, no
objects other than the pebbles and grass tufts of tile 16.
