A 768x256 pixel-art WALL set for a top-down 2D MMORPG, on a solid flat magenta
background (#FF00FF) that becomes transparency. Clean hand-drawn pixel art,
NOT a photo, NOT flat — the walls have a strong 3D BEVEL.

STYLE (this is the important part): the walls are smooth stone rails with a
chiselled 3D bevel, exactly like the classic Tibia / RPG-Maker wall look.
Each wall is a raised ridge: a bright highlight along the top-left edge, a
mid-grey top surface, and a darker face falling away to the bottom-right, so
the wall looks like it pops UP out of the floor. Rounded, polished stone —
smooth bevel, not rough bricks, only faint block seams. Light from the
north-west. 4-tone grey ramp: #4a4f57 shadow, #6b7079, #939aa3, #c4c9d0
highlight. Around each wall the cell is flat pure magenta, hard clean edge,
no shadow onto the magenta.

LAYOUT: exactly 3 cells in one row, each cell 256x256 pixels, side by side,
no gaps or lines between them.

SAME HEIGHT — MOST IMPORTANT: in all three cells the wall ridge must have the
EXACT same thickness and sit on the EXACT same pixel rows. The top highlight is
on the same row in every cell and the bottom is on the same row in every cell.
Do not make one thicker, thinner, taller or shifted. If they differ the walls
will not line up in the game.

The three pieces, left to right:

CELL 1 — HORIZONTAL WALL: a straight beveled ridge running left-to-right across
the full width, centred vertically. It must TILE horizontally: the left edge
continues perfectly into the right edge.

CELL 2 — VERTICAL WALL: a straight beveled ridge running top-to-bottom across
the full height, centred horizontally. It must TILE vertically: the top edge
continues perfectly into the bottom edge. Same ridge thickness as cell 1.

CELL 3 — POLE / CORNER POST: a single short beveled post, the connector where
walls meet. Its ridge is the same thickness as the other two, centred in the
cell, so that a horizontal wall + this pole + a vertical wall snap into a clean
right-angle corner with no gap and no overlap.

All three drawn at the same scale, same bevel, same light, so they read as one
continuous wall.

Strict: no text, no numbers, no watermark, no floor or grass under the walls
(only magenta), no characters, no props, no doors, no windows.
