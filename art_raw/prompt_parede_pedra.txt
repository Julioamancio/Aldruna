A 1024x256 pixel-art WALL set for a top-down 2D MMORPG, on a solid flat magenta
background (#FF00FF) that becomes transparency. Clean hand-drawn pixel art with
a strong 3D BEVEL — NOT a photo, NOT flat.

STYLE: smooth stone rails with a chiselled 3D bevel, the classic Tibia wall
look. Each wall is a raised ridge: bright highlight along the top-left edge, a
light-grey top surface, and a darker face falling to the bottom-right, so the
wall pops UP from the floor. Smooth polished stone, only faint seams. Light
from the north-west. 4-tone grey ramp: #4a4f57 shadow, #6b7079, #939aa3,
#c4c9d0 highlight. Around every wall the cell is flat pure magenta with a hard
clean edge — no shadow onto the magenta.

LAYOUT: exactly 4 cells in ONE row, each cell 256x256 pixels, side by side,
no gaps or lines between them.

SAME RIDGE — MOST IMPORTANT: the wall ridge has the EXACT same thickness in all
four cells, and where a ridge reaches a cell edge it sits on the EXACT same
pixel rows/columns as the matching ridge in the neighbour piece. This is what
lets the pieces snap together. Do not vary the thickness or shift the ridge.

The four pieces, left to right:

CELL 1 — HORIZONTAL WALL: one straight ridge running left-to-right across the
full width, centred vertically. Tiles horizontally (left edge continues into
right edge).

CELL 2 — VERTICAL WALL: one straight ridge running top-to-bottom across the
full height, centred horizontally. Tiles vertically. Same ridge thickness as
cell 1.

CELL 3 — CORNER: an L-shaped ridge where a horizontal wall and a vertical wall
meet at a right angle (one arm goes to the right edge, one arm goes to the
bottom edge). The arms are the same thickness as cells 1 and 2 and meet in a
clean rounded corner.

CELL 4 — POLE / JUNCTION POST: a single short beveled post, the connector
placed where walls cross. Same ridge thickness, centred, so horizontal +
vertical + corner + pole all snap together with no gap and no overlap.

All four drawn at the same scale, same bevel, same light, so they read as one
continuous wall system.

Strict: no text, no numbers, no watermark, no floor or grass under the walls
(only magenta), no characters, no props, no doors, no windows.
