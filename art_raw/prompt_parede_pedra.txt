A 512x512 pixel-art WALL tileset for a top-down 2D MMORPG in the polished
16-bit style of A Link to the Past and Chrono Trigger, drawn over a solid pure
magenta background (#FF00FF) that will be turned into transparency.

LAYOUT: a 4x4 grid of 16 cells, each cell exactly 128x128 pixels. Each cell
holds one wall piece. No grid lines or labels between cells.

RESOLUTION: each piece is authored as 32x32 pixel art shown at 4x, so one art
pixel is a crisp 4x4 block. Hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no
photographic texture. Fine detail must still read at 32x32.

THESE ARE WALLS SEEN IN THE TIBIA 3/4 PERSPECTIVE: the wall stands UP from the
floor. The base of the wall sits at the bottom of the cell; the wall face
rises toward the top of the cell. The very top edge catches light; the face is
mid-tone grey stone blocks; a thin dark line at the bottom grounds it. Outside
the wall shape, the cell is flat pure magenta with a hard edge — no shadow or
glow onto the magenta.

Material for every piece: grey cut-stone blocks (mortar lines between them),
palette #3a3a3a #4f4f4f #6b6b6b #868686 #a3a3a3, lit from the north-west.

The 16 pieces, in reading order, must connect seamlessly with each other:
1. straight horizontal wall (runs left-right, full width)
2. straight vertical wall (runs top-bottom, full height)
3. corner: wall coming from the south turning to the east
4. corner: wall coming from the south turning to the west
5. corner: wall coming from the north turning to the east
6. corner: wall coming from the north turning to the west
7. T-junction opening to the south
8. T-junction opening to the north
9. T-junction opening to the east
10. T-junction opening to the west
11. cross junction (four ways)
12. short horizontal end cap (wall ending, open to the west)
13. short vertical end cap (wall ending, open to the north)
14. single free-standing pillar/column
15. horizontal wall with a wooden door in the middle
16. horizontal wall with a barred window in the middle

Strict: no text, no numbers, no watermark, no characters, no floor texture
under the wall (only magenta around the wall), no props.
