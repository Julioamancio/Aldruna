Create a 1024x1024 pixel art tile sheet for a 2D top-down MMORPG, arranged as a
4x4 grid of 16 square tiles (each tile is exactly 256x256 pixels). These are
WATER EDGE OVERLAY tiles: each tile shows the edge of a water area, drawn over
a solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF) which will be removed and replaced by
transparency.

Only the water itself is drawn; the rest of every tile must be flat pure
magenta with a hard edge - no fading, no shadow, no glow onto the magenta.

CRITICAL ALIGNMENT RULE: every shape must be positioned exactly as described
and must touch the tile edges it owns, reaching all the way to the border of
its 256x256 cell. Do not shrink, offset or center shapes that should touch the
edges.

The 16 tiles, in reading order:
1. water filling the top half, wavy horizontal shoreline across the middle
2. water filling the right half, wavy vertical shoreline
3. water filling the bottom half, wavy horizontal shoreline
4. water filling the left half, wavy vertical shoreline
5. water filling only the top-right quarter (outer corner, curved shoreline)
6. water filling only the bottom-right quarter (outer corner)
7. water filling only the bottom-left quarter (outer corner)
8. water filling only the top-left quarter (outer corner)
9. water filling everything except the top-right quarter (inner corner)
10. water filling everything except the bottom-right quarter (inner corner)
11. water filling everything except the bottom-left quarter (inner corner)
12. water filling everything except the top-left quarter (inner corner)
13. horizontal water strip across the middle (a narrow channel)
14. vertical water strip down the middle (a narrow channel)
15. small round pond in the centre, isolated
16. water edge with two or three tiny wet rocks along the shoreline

The water must match this palette exactly: #3f7d94 #4d92aa #255a70, with a thin
line of pale foam #d9e8ec exactly along the shoreline, one or two pixels wide,
slightly irregular so it reads as gentle lapping water, never a clean geometric
line. Inside the water, a few subtle ripple lines in the lighter tone.

Lighting comes from the north-west: the north-west side of the water is
slightly lighter, the south-east side slightly darker, but never pure black.

Style: crisp pixel art, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no
gradients, flat lighting. Strict rules: no text, no labels, no numbers, no
watermark, no grid lines between tiles, no drop shadows, no fish, no boats, no
characters.
